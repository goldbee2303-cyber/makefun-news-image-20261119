Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 19 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

A dense newspaper-style image summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026 — 15 stories on one page: a navy masthead with the date, a "THE DAY IN UTC" timeline, three illustrated lead stories, eight cards, a numbered briefs strip, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" map and a "DATE CHECK" footer.

Leads: the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen ends in a four-point "Declaration toward Peace"; the ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, 9–6; the Kerch Bridge is hit again overnight, rail damaged. Cards: the US Senate rejects the Iran war-powers resolution 51–48; Cyclone Akvan nears India with 46,700 evacuated; five aid agencies score the Gaza peace plan 4/26 after a year; an Israeli operation crosses the "yellow line" in Deir al-Balah, 18,000+ displaced; a Houthi missile hits Riyadh, damaging the PIF tower; Denmark approves the Greenland–US defence deal 109–0; APEC adopts the "Shenzhen Outlook"; the UK and Norway fund two Ukrainian warships. Briefs: COP31 final-stage talks; Muhammad Yunus's ancestral home burned in Bangladesh; the LPG tanker Gas Husky sank off Bulgaria; the IAEA says North Korea is expanding uranium enrichment at Yongbyon.

Built in the dense front-page style of the 18 November entry — timeline strip, detailed flat illustrations per story, numbered briefs, world map and date check — using only verified facts. The image is drawn as a front page and the program copies it to the output directory unchanged.

Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. The image is 1024×1024 PNG.
